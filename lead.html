01Leadership

Culture and trust shape organizations long before strategy does.

Loretta works with leaders on strategy, organizational culture and executive leadership — the values, behaviour and trust that decide whether a strategy ever leaves the slide deck.

Her leadership was forged across sectors: as a creative director leading campaigns for LVMH-Moët Hennessy, Louis Vuitton, Samsung, Nike and BNP Paribas; facilitating a roundtable with the Chairman of Temasek Holdings; and leading leadership development with executives across Bhutan’s state-owned enterprises for Druk Holding & Investments.
