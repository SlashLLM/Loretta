03 · Lead

Lead with
trust.

Global Executive · Cultural Strategist Strategy · Culture · Executive leadership · Advisory
01Leadership

Strategy sets direction. Trust eats strategy for breakfast.

Loretta works with leaders and organizations navigating growth, transformation and uncertainty bringing together strategy, organizational culture, innovation and the human dynamics that determine whether change actually happens.

Her perspective comes from having led from radically different seats: founder and CEO, regional executive, cultural institution leader, professor, creative director and adviser. Across technology, education, culture and enterprise across Asia and the United States. She knows that the hardest organizational problems are rarely solved by strategy alone.

They are solved by people: trust, clarity, courage, accountability and the ability to move together.

Her leadership philosophy is grounded in a simple belief:

Trust is infrastructure.

01

Strategy

Turning complexity into direction.

Helping organizations see what matters, make choices and translate ambitious ideas into action, particularly at moments of growth, reinvention and disruption.

02

Culture & trust

Building conditions for people to thrive.

Culture is not perks or slogans. It is how decisions get made, disagreement gets handled, information travels and trust is earned especially when nobody is watching.

03

Leadership & transformation

Leading through change without losing the people.

From startups to established institutions, Loretta works at the intersection of transformation and humanity, aligning people around purpose while creating the courage and accountability needed to move.

04

Global advisory

Bridging Asia, America and the Pacific.

Strategic counsel, executive education and ecosystem building for organizations navigating innovation, cross-cultural leadership and opportunities across markets.

02 · Track record

Built. Led. Transformed.

Three decades across technology, enterprise, education, culture and the creative industries as founder and CEO, regional executive, educator, institutional leader and strategic adviser.

Loretta has built companies and ventures, led multidisciplinary teams across markets, advised and developed senior leaders, and created work for global organizations including LVMH–Moët Hennessy, Louis Vuitton, Samsung, Nike and BNP Paribas.

Today, her work sits increasingly at the intersection of AI, entrepreneurship, organizational transformation and technology for good by connecting ideas, people and opportunities across Asia, Hawaiʻi and the United States.
