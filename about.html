01The short version

Global Executive. Technopreneur. Educator. Author. Cultural Strategist. A builder of ideas, organizations and communities.

Dr. Loretta Chen operates where technology, leadership, culture and human possibility intersect. She is the co-founder and CEO of Smobler, teaches strategy, leadership and entrepreneurship at universities and in executive education, and is a Straits Times bestselling author. Originally from Singapore, she now lives in East Honolulu, Hawai‘i — five minutes from the ocean, with a huge garden and eighteen rescue cats.

She holds a doctorate in theatre studies from the National University of Singapore and UCLA, and has also studied at UC Davis and the University of London.

Her work spans four worlds: Build — Smobler, AI, emerging tech, entrepreneurship and venture building; Lead — strategy, organizational culture, executive leadership and advisory; Teach — universities, executive education, students and workshops; and Create — books, theatre, culture, speaking and storytelling.

Across all four sits one idea: from margins to frontier — the edge as territory. Women whose stories aren’t told. Emerging technologies before they become mainstream. Students finding their voices. ASEAN leaders beyond the official biography. Hawai‘i’s immigrant histories. Artists and entrepreneurs. Technology for underserved communities. Ideas moving between Asia and America.

03The stage years

Before the boardroom came the stage — telling the stories the mainstream leaves in the wings.

As a theatre and creative director, Loretta went where polite audiences flinch — from 251 to The Vagina Monologues and The F Word, which earned her the Amnesty International Freedom of Expression Award. She carried the same instinct into campaigns for LVMH-Moët Hennessy, Louis Vuitton, Samsung, Nike and BNP Paribas, and onto the airwaves as host of The Art of Lush on Lush 99.5FM. It is the grounding for how she leads today: culture first, story always.

06 · Off duty

My perfect weekend

“My perfect weekend is waking up to see the sunrise. The day will start at about 3 or 4am.”

As told to The Straits Times · 9 Feb 2023

3–4 AM Sunrise at Makapu‘u A drive by the ocean up to the eastern tip of O‘ahu, then back down to take in the beauty of Hawai‘i.
Dawn Meditate. Coffee. Vanilla macadamia by Roy’s, then a mochi waffle at Koa Pancake House. (In Singapore: prata, lontong, or mee pok with lots of chilli.)
Morning Read Leadership, humanity, technology and culture — Ray Dalio, Ben Horowitz, Mustafa Suleyman, Erin Meyer. Always mid-way through several. See the nightstand ↗
Noon Lunch with 18 cats “Not as blissful as it sounds because they will want food.”
Afternoon 30 minutes on the bike Prof G, Planet Money, TED and the BBC in her ears — or David Guetta and Calvin Harris.
Night Lights out Four or five cats in the bed, “particularly Ningje, who always puts his butt on my face.”
